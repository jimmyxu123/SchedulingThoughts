Scheduling Thoughts

Learning the Order of Thought in Diffusion Language Models

Jiawei Xu1 Minghui Liu1 Aakriti Agrawal1 Yifan Chen2 Furong Huang1
1 University of Maryland 2 UCLA
Paper Code

Abstract

Masked diffusion language models (MDMs) decode by iteratively unmasking tokens. While the sequence of unmasking, the "order of thought," crucially impacts generation quality, it is usually determined by simple heuristics. We introduce Self-Aware Scheduling (SAS), a method that learns a lightweight order policy using Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO).

Key Contributions

  • Principled Policy Optimization: We treat unmasking order as a reinforcement learning problem.
  • SAS Framework: Learns a lightweight policy for a frozen denoiser.
  • Performance Gains: Significant improvements in Sudoku accuracy and mathematical reasoning.
